## Product concept
[An easy to use marketplace for buyers and sellers. Sites like Amazon, Ebay and Temu, although popular are very cluttered and unfocused. This is my iteration of the concept which is supposed to be simple but not lacking in any features. The workflow and UX should be smooth]

## Design direction (make this distinct from Amazon)
-Visual identity: Friendly, high-energy marketplace aesthetic — rounded geometry throughout (16–20px card radii, pill-shaped buttons and search bar), soft elevation shadows instead of hard borders, and a "colorful modular blocks" system where each promo/category section gets its own accent color (coral, sky blue, mint, mustard) against a neutral white/off-white base. This keeps the page visually lively without relying on product photography alone to create energy.

Typography: A rounded-geometric sans-serif for headings (something like Poppins, Manrope, or Plus Jakarta Sans) paired with a clean grotesque for body/UI text (Inter or similar). Headings bold and slightly oversized on promo banners; UI text small and tight in product cards to keep density high.

Layout:
Sticky top bar: logo left, pill-shaped search bar centered/expanded, cart + account icons right
Horizontal category rail (circular icon + label) directly under the header instead of a sidebar tree
Homepage built from stacked "modules" — hero banner carousel, then alternating full-width colored promo blocks and product grids — rather than one continuous feed
Product cards: white background, rounded corners, product image on soft tinted backdrop, title + price + star rating stacked tightly, small discount badge top-left

Color system:
Light mode: white/
#FAFAFA base, dark near-black text (
#1A1A1A), and the colorful blocks (coral 
#FF6B6B-ish, blue 
#4B7BFF-ish, mint, mustard) used as section backgrounds and CTA buttons — never as body text color, to keep contrast clean
Dark mode: base shifts to a deep charcoal/near-black (
#121212–
#181818), cards become a slightly lighter elevated surface (
#1F1F1F–
#242424) with subtle 1px hairline borders instead of shadows (shadows read poorly on dark), text inverts to off-white (
#F2F2F2), and the accent colors desaturate slightly (10–15%) so they don't vibrate against the dark background — e.g. coral shifts toward a muted rose, mint toward a deeper teal
Accent colors stay consistent in hue between modes (same brand palette), only their saturation/lightness shifts for dark-mode legibility
Motion: Card hover = slight lift (translateY + shadow increase in light mode, border-glow in dark mode); category icons scale slightly on hover; smooth 200–250ms transitions on theme toggle so light/dark doesn't feel like a hard cut.
Theme toggle: Persist user preference (localStorage or account setting), default to system preference on first visit.
- Mobile-first responsive design
